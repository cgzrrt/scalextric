Origin / AI disclosure
----------------------
Method: procedural (no photos, no AI-generated textures or images, AI-assisted code)
The texture was built procedurally from Blender shader nodes and baked in Blender. Normal maps, ambient
occlusion and the smaller sizes were calculated from the baked height and colour with Python scripts.
The scripts were written with the help of an AI coding assistant. No generative AI model produced the
textures or images directly, and no photos were used.
If your platform asks whether AI was used in the creation process, note that the scripts were written with an
AI coding assistant.
